Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Nsam Technologies runs a computer-based practice portal for midwifery and nursing students. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name and the username given to you for the portal.
  • Your exam activity: the papers you attempt, your answers, your scores, and how many times you log in.
  • If you use the Notify me box, the name and WhatsApp number you send us through WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To let you log in and write practice papers.
  • To show you your results and review.
  • To manage access, for example switching a login on or off.
  • To tell you when new exams, such as the nursing papers, are available.

Who can see it

Only the site admin can see student records. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. Your data is stored with our technology provider, Google Firebase, which keeps it on secure servers.

How long we keep it

We keep your records while the portal is in use for your class. You can ask us to delete your record at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information by messaging us on WhatsApp at +234 708 106 4223.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
