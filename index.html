Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you log in.

How do I log in?

Use the username and password on your slip. Usernames look like DOM/3Y1S/05 and the password is 3 letters. Capital or small letters do not matter.

What is the paper PIN?

Each paper has its own PIN. After you log in, choose your paper, enter its PIN and tap Proceed to Exam Room.

How long is each paper?

Each paper has 250 questions and a 3-hour timer, like the real exam. The paper submits itself when the time ends.

Will I lose my answers if my phone network drops?

Your answers are saved as you go. If your result does not save at the end, tap Retry saving my result once your network is back.

When do I see my score?

Straight after you submit. You will see your result, the correct answers, and an explanation for the questions you missed.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. The portal works on phones, tablets and computers.

Will the questions change?

Yes. The questions in each paper are refreshed every few days, so check back and practise again.

I forgot my password or my login says it is switched off. What do I do?

Message the admin on WhatsApp at +234 708 106 4223 with your full name and username.

Is there a nursing exam?

It is coming soon. Use the Notify me box on the Nursing card and we will message you when it opens.

Is my information safe?

We only keep what the portal needs, such as your name, username and scores. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
